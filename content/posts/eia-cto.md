## Situation

A high-intensity programme with a multinational team tasked with shipping a purpose-driven product on a 21-day runway.

## Task

Lead the team to develop an MVP for a social matching platform and complete product definition with market validation.

## Action

- Led a multinational team through product definition, delivery, and validation within the programme window.
- Designed algorithms aligning users’ interests and itineraries to address loneliness and safety for solo travellers.
- Architected user flows and optimised security verification to reflect social preferences and trust requirements.

## Result

Delivered an MVP with clear product narrative and validation outcomes within the 21-day timeline.
