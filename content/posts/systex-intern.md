Client names, system details, and business logic are confidential, so this page stays general.

I documented about 6 interviews and turned each into flow diagrams and functional specifications. My CS background helped me judge early whether a request was technically possible, and break it into steps engineers could build.

My rule for a specification was simple. An engineer should be able to build from any page without coming back to ask. That meant settling the page flows, the field logic, and the exception scenarios on paper, before anything reached the engineering team.

With my mentor I co-wrote a 400–500 page functional specification. In a practice assignment from another mentor, I worked out the cross-role notification and settings logic on my own. For testing, I used an AI skill to generate SIT/UAT cases in batches, then checked every case by hand. It cut about two weeks of writing to about two days.
