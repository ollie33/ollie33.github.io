## Situation

Client-facing projects needed tight alignment between business needs, user flows, and what engineering could ship on schedule.

## Task

Gather and translate requirements into a clear scope; author SRS/FSD as the single source of truth; validate defects against specifications.

## Action

- Spearheaded requirements gathering through client interviews and translated outcomes into user flows and feature roadmaps.
- Authored System Requirements Specifications (SRS) and Functional Specifications Documents (FSD), defining logic and UI fields for the development team.
- Executed logic validation and root-cause analysis for bugs to keep deliverables aligned with technical specifications.

## Result

Delivered a well-defined project scope and documentation foundation that enabled engineering to build with clarity and traceability.
