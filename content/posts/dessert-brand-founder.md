## Situation

Operating an independent brand end-to-end with limited bandwidth for repetitive admin work.

## Task

Grow the brand while controlling cost, quality, and time spent on operations.

## Action

- Managed brand building, product development, and cost control across the full lifecycle.
- Automated administrative workflows by integrating Gmail and Google Sheets via n8n, parsing order emails with AI.
- Applied iterative testing from product development to technical troubleshooting, using data feedback to steer decisions.

## Result

Demonstrated execution from business to technology; reduced administrative time by roughly 80%.
