Hello, I'm **Ying-Hsien Lee** — a **B.S. in Computer Science** from **Feng Chia University** (FCU Honor Student), working at the point where product meets engineering.

Ten months at **SYSTEX** running requirement interviews and writing specifications. A four-person **RAG system** I led to publication at **TCSE 2025**. A **dessert brand** I built and ran alone for seventeen months.

Three unrelated things, one repeated lesson: *most problems aren't hard to build — they were never defined properly to begin with.* Finding the actual problem is the work I want to keep doing.

## How I Use AI

Not a tool list — each project here used AI at a different layer of the work:

- **Built an AI system** — designed and benchmarked a RAG pipeline (FAISS, bge-m3) that grounded LLM output in retrieved fact; published at TCSE 2025.
- **Shipped with AI** — used Cursor to compress a 15-day MVP build, trading implementation hours for problem-definition time at EIA.
- **Operated with AI** — wired Gemini into an n8n pipeline to parse messy order emails into clean structured data.
- **Verified AI's work** — generated SIT/UAT test cases at volume, then personally reviewed every case, because an unreviewed suite gives you confidence you haven't earned.
